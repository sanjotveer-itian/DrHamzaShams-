About Dr. Humza Shams

General Practitioner and Sonologist.

Healthy you, happier tomorrow

MBBSRMPSonologist

Dr. Humza Shams combines general practice with diagnostic ultrasound. That means one doctor can examine you, scan you and explain the result in the same visit.

He sees patients at Al Humza Clinic on weekends and at the LUMHS Diagnostic and Research Laboratory on weekdays, so care is available every day of the week.
